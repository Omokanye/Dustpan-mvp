How it works

  1. CollectGather clean, dry plastic bottles, containers and cans.
  2. Drop offBring them to a Dustpan drop-off point and give staff your Customer ID.
  3. VerifyStaff weigh and check your waste.
  4. EarnDUST is added to your wallet. You can see it in Activity.

What we accept

  • PET bottles
  • Other plastics
  • Aluminium cans

Not sure about something? Ask staff at the drop-off point.

You can't add DUST yourself. Only waste that staff have weighed and verified is credited.
